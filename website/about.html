The Founder

Eli's Story

Eli isn't a coach who quit trading to sell courses. He's a prop trader who built Sin Miedo Capital from scratch — out of frustration, discipline, and a refusal to keep guessing.

Diagnosed with ADHD, he didn't beat the markets by forcing himself into someone else's rigid playbook. He built a system that works with the way his mind operates — structured enough to remove noise, flexible enough to trust.

Today he trades MES futures live, every session, using the SMC Venice + NY Engine system. He keeps it real — wins, losses, and everything in between — because that's the only way anyone actually learns.
